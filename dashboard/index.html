How the implied value is computed model radar-2

implied = TVL × 0.30 × (1 + 0.6 × clamped 7-day growth) × (0.5 + 0.5 × min(age, 3y) / 3y)

  • TVL × 0.30 — the base. A token's market cap usually sits at 0.2–0.4× the value its protocol secures; 0.30 is the middle of that band.
  • Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, weighted 0.6. A growing protocol gains users, so its token would price higher; a shrinking one lower.
  • Age without a token — 0.5 at launch, rising to 1.0 after three years. A protocol that has run for years with no token has a longer record to price and a larger unclaimed audience.

The same formula scores every row. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the ranking comes only from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day growth and time without a token. Protocols whose token already trades are listed separately at the market's own value, never mixed into this ranking. A protocol on this board is recorded as having no token; that is what its DefiLlama record says, not proof it has none.
